Who we are

Growing health, opportunity, and sustainability.

Agri-Health Hub Rwanda is an agricultural business project focused on growing and selling green vegetables while exploring sustainable ways to turn biodegradable waste into organic manure.

Our mission is to improve access to nutritious food, create opportunities in our community, and build a business that respects the environment.

What the indicators suggest

  • Organic fertiliser use was widely reported in both seasons.
  • Improved seed use differed between Season A and Season B.
  • Only 13.4% of farmers practised irrigation in Season A 2025, highlighting an area to explore for water-efficient production.
  • 90.3% of farmers practised anti-erosion activities in Season A 2025.

These are descriptive survey findings. They do not prove that a particular input caused higher yields.

Data sources & transparency

NISR · Seasonal Agricultural Survey Season A 2026NISR · Seasonal Agricultural Survey Annual Report 2025NISR · Rwanda Seasonal Agricultural Survey 2025 microdata catalogueNISR · Seasonal Agricultural Survey programme

Data status: The dashboard combines published national summary indicators from NISR SAS Season A 2026 and selected 2025 season comparisons. It does not yet download, clean or analyse the microdata, and it does not provide district-level results. The calculators use values entered by the visitor. No predictive AI model, live sensor, satellite feed, weather API or automated farmer alert is claimed as implemented.

AI-use disclosure: AI assistance was used to help draft and code parts of the website prototype and explanatory text. The team must review, test, verify all figures and sources, and take responsibility for the final submission.
