Who we are

Growing health, opportunity, and sustainability.

Agri-Health Hub Rwanda is an agricultural business project focused on growing and selling green vegetables while exploring sustainable ways to turn biodegradable waste into organic manure.

Our mission is to improve access to nutritious food, create opportunities in our community, and build a business that respects the environment.
